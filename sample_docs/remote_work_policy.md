# Northwind Remote Work Policy

Effective 1 March 2025. Owner: People Operations.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their six-month probation period are eligible for hybrid work. Employees in their probation period must work from an office location. Roles that require physical presence, such as warehouse operations and lab technicians, are not eligible for remote work.

## Hybrid schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Every team must designate two anchor days, Tuesday and Thursday by default, when all team members are expected to be in the office. A manager can approve a different anchor-day schedule for a team in writing.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for a maximum of 20 working days per calendar year, with prior approval from their manager and the People Operations team at least 30 days in advance. Working abroad for longer creates tax and immigration obligations for the company, so longer stays require approval from the Chief People Officer and the Legal team. Employees must never work from a sanctioned country.

## Home office stipend

Northwind provides a one-time home office stipend of 600 EUR for new hires who are eligible for hybrid work. After that, a yearly allowance of 150 EUR can be claimed for ergonomic equipment. Receipts must be submitted through the Expenses portal within 60 days of purchase.

## Internet and equipment

Northwind reimburses up to 40 EUR per month of home internet costs for employees who work remotely at least two days a week. Company laptops must be used for all work; personal computers may not be used to access production systems.

## Core hours

Regardless of location, everyone is expected to be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone. Meetings with participants across time zones should be scheduled inside the overlap of these windows where possible.
